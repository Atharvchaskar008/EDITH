# How much the ranking depends on our assumptions

We ranked 175 close passes by worst-case probability, then changed one assumption at a time. Halving or doubling the uncertainty leaves the worst-case ranking untouched (10 and 10 of the top 10 stay, 0 passes change level): that is why we rank by it. Changing the object size from 10 m to 5 m or 20 m keeps 7 and 7 of the top 10 but moves 38 and 16 passes across a level. Swapping measured uncertainty for a guessed table keeps 7 of the top 10 (rank agreement 0.67 for the worst case, 0.46 for the plain probability). Closeness alone agrees with the worst-case ranking at 0.31. Example: STARLINK-37021 / COSMOS 2251 DEB passes at 141 m yet has 497 times the worst-case probability of STARLINK-37785 / STARLINK-38249 at 87 m, because its miss lies along the long axis of the uncertainty while the other's lies between the axes.
